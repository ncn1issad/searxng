Crucial legal milestone today for Kenya's wildlife: The High Court has issued a permanent injunction safeguarding the Kimana wildlife corridor from industrial subdivision. Why does this matter? A short breakdown on connectivity 🧵 (1/4)

Dr. Paula Kahumbu author 4 hours ago

1/ Over 2,200 elephants use this 4-kilometer wide passage to move between Amboseli, the Chyulu Hills, and Tsavo West. Without it, the ecosystem splits in two, isolating populations and sparking acute human-elephant conflict with perimeter smallholders.

Dr. Paula Kahumbu author 4 hours ago

2/ The ruling reinforces community conservancy leases as legally binding land covenants. Maasai landowners who set aside grazing lands for wildlife tourism now have judicial backing against speculative developers.

Acoustic analysis confirms wild African elephants use distinct name-like vocal labels for individual herd members

Our lab analyzed 469 low-frequency acoustic rumbles across Samburu and Amboseli reserves over 14 months. When playback experiments were conducted, target elephants responded immediately by vocalizing and moving toward the speaker, while non-target elephants showed no orientation response. This demonstrates arbitrary acoustic naming rather than simple maternal tone recognition.
