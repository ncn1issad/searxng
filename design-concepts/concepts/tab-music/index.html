Daft Punk

French electronic music duo · Paris, France · 1993 – 2021

Daft Punk was a French electronic music duo formed in 1993 in Paris by Thomas Bangalter and Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo. Widely regarded as one of the most influential acts in dance music history, they achieved critical acclaim with their distinctive visual identity, helmet personas, and synthesised sound.
